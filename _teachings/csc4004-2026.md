## Course Overview

At the end of the course a student should be able to:

- Produce a report of the practical work done over one academic year.
- Prove that they have acquired the necessary techniques for carrying quality practical work in their chosen field. This should be reflected in the report.

## Prerequisites

All second and third year courses. In particular, a specific optional course may be required depending on the chosen project.

## Grading

- Research Proposal Writing: 15%
- Research Proposal Oral Presentation: 5%
- Project Implementation and Dissertation/Report: 70%
- Oral Presentation of the Project: 10%
